## 2. Title & Subtitle Strategy

App stores put the highest algorithmic weight on the **App Title** and **Short Description/Subtitle**.

### Google Play Store Strategy
*   **App Title (Max 30 chars):** `DueVault: Bill & Doc Scanner` (28 chars)
    *   *Why:* "DueVault" establishes the brand name. "Bill & Doc Scanner" immediately targets the high-intent keywords *bill scanner* and *doc scanner*.
*   **Short Description (Max 80 chars):** `Scan receipts, track expenses, organize bills & sync securely to your cloud.` (76 chars)
    *   *Why:* Mentions 4 high-value verbs (*Scan*, *track*, *organize*, *sync*) and 4 key nouns (*receipts*, *expenses*, *bills*, *cloud*).

### Apple App Store Strategy
*   **App Name (Max 30 chars):** `DueVault: Secure Bill Tracker` (29 chars)
    *   *Why:* Connects brand to *secure bill tracker*, which is highly relevant for iOS search terms.
*   **Subtitle (Max 30 chars):** `Secure Bill & Document Vault` (28 chars)
    *   *Why:* Matches *secure*, *bill*, *document*, and *vault*.
*   **Keyword List (Max 100 chars, comma-separated, no spaces):**
    `finance,expense,tracker,budget,bill,organizer,scanner,receipt,secure,vault,pdf,ocr,offline,sync,lock`

## 3. SEO Formatting & Full Description Best Practices

*   **Keyword Density:** The core keywords (`receipt scanner`, `expense tracker`, `bill organizer`, `secure vault`) should appear 3 to 4 times in the description. Avoid "keyword stuffing" (repeating them without grammatical sense), as Google Play penalizes it.
*   **Clear Hierarchy:** Use clear headings, bullet points, and capitalization to make the description scannable for both users and algorithms.
*   **The Hook:** Start with a powerful 2-3 sentence statement explaining *exactly* what the app does and why the user needs it.
*   **Feature List:** Detail specific use cases (e.g., auto-filling with OCR, biometric secure folders, Google Drive sync).
*   **PRO Features Callout:** List what benefits the one-time PRO purchase unlocks (e.g., unlimited uploads, OCR scanner, encrypted database).

## 4. Release Management & App Store Maintenance

ASO is not a set-and-forget task. To maintain high organic rankings:
1.  **A/B Test Store Elements:** Regularly test different screenshots and short descriptions using Google Play Console's Store Listing Experiments.
2.  **Encourage Reviews:** Use prompt packages to ask satisfied users for 5-star ratings. Respond to reviews promptly on Google Play Console (Google's algorithm favors active developers).
3.  **Release Notes Optimization:** When updating the app, write meaningful "What's New" logs, keeping them engaging instead of just saying "bug fixes".
